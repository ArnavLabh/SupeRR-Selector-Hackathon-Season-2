# Product context: Pressure Index dashboard

register: product

## Purpose
Show who performs under pressure in T20 cricket, why the model says so, and what it means for
Rajasthan Royals' IPL 2027 auction. It accompanies a methodology report submitted to the
SupeRR Selector Season 2 competition.

## Users
- **Evaluators and franchise decision makers.** Open the link on a laptop in a lit room, often with the
  PDF beside it. They want the two top-10 lists and the reason for each name within a minute.
- **Analysts.** Want to interrogate: definitions, sample sizes, uncertainty, robustness, and any
  individual player's record.

## Scene
A selector at a desk in daylight, laptop open, checking a name from the report against the evidence,
then projecting the same page in an auction-planning meeting. Light surface, high legibility, prints well.

## Tone
A scouting department's internal tool: precise, calm, confident about what is known and plain about
what is not. Cricket vocabulary, not statistics jargon, in the first layer; the statistics one click down.

## Brand
Rajasthan Royals inspired, not a replica: deep navy ink, Royals pink as the single accent, gold used
only to mark rank or distinction. No team logos or official marks.

## Anti-references
- Dark neon "esports" dashboards.
- Grids of identical KPI cards with big numbers and no context.
- Decorative charts: gauges, 3D, radar charts with unreadable axes.
- Fan-site styling: gradients, glows, player cut-outs.

## Principles
1. Every number shows its uncertainty or its sample size.
2. Evidence a cricketer trusts (strike rate, economy, balls faced) sits next to every model estimate.
3. The primary ranking and the auction opinion are visibly separate things.
4. Nothing is shown that cannot be traced to a file in `outputs/`.
